# Net Atlas — legal notice

**Status.** Net Atlas gives indicative estimates for typical circumstances. It is not tax, legal, accounting, payroll, employment or credit advice
and does not replace a payslip, a tax return, an official decision or a lender's pre-contract information. The estimate of a benefit is not a
decision on entitlement: the paying institution decides on your actual record.

**Who provides it.** The NeoBankers Group Limited is a holding company. It is not a bank, does not provide payroll, tax-advisory or credit services
through this tool and does not carry on regulated activities through it.

**Method and sources.** Each calculation is based on the official rules of the country for the tax year shown on the result, with the date and
time of calculation and the version of the rules stamped on every result. Values not yet confirmed by an official source are shown as provisional.
The list of official sources for each calculation is shown on the result page.

**Privacy.** Calculations run on your device. What you enter is not sent to the Group. The application keeps your last inputs, favourite countries
and theme in your browser's local storage so it can reopen where you left off; clear your browser data to remove them.

**Errors and corrections.** If you find a rule that is out of date or wrong, tell us through https://theneobankers.group/enquiries/general.html
(choose the Net Atlas project). We review reported changes and update the rules.

**Third-party names** belong to their owners. Exchange rates are European Central Bank reference rates, shown with their date.
